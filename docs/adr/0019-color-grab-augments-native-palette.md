# 0019. Colour grabber augments phpBB's own colour palette

Status: Accepted

Date: 2026-07-24

## Context

On a reply page phpBB renders a **topic review** (`#topicreview`) below the composer — the
thread's recent posts, with each character's speech in an inline `<span style="color:#…">`.
Roleplayers constantly reuse one another's colours and today copy the hex out of raw BBCode by
hand. Feature #4 ("colour grabber") exists to remove that friction.

The obvious shape is a new toolbar button opening our own popup that lists the review's colours
(who used each, how often) and inserts `[color=#…]` on click — the same shadow-DOM-Svelte
surface as the bbcode-presets menu. Two things pushed against it:

- **There is already a place for picking a colour.** phpBB's `bbcode-color` button toggles a
  font-colour palette (`#colour_palette`) — a fixed grid of swatches that already insert
  `[color=#RRGGBB]…[/color]`. A second, parallel colour UI a few pixels away is redundant and
  another button in a crowded toolbar.
- **Attribution can't be made precise.** A colour in a post may be the author's own or one they
  quoted — with or without a `[quote]` tag, sometimes reproduced from a page we can't see. No
  heuristic reliably tells "owns" from "quotes".

A constraint discovered while reading the live page shaped the mechanism: the palette grid is
**generated by phpBB's own JS** (`registerPalette` in core.js) on DOM-ready — it replaces the
server-rendered `<table>` inside `#color_palette_placeholder` wholesale and binds each swatch's
click *per-anchor*. A content script may run before or after that.

The palette grid is also **fixed**: every channel is one of `00/40/80/BF/FF`. That is precisely
where players pick speech colours, so in practice review colours already have a cell — but a
hand-typed `[color=#123456]` will not.

## Decision

We will **augment phpBB's palette rather than build our own colour surface**. The feature injects
a single checkbox ("Sur la page") into `#colour_palette`. When ticked:

- swatches whose colour is not used in the review are hidden with `visibility:hidden` (kept in
  layout, so the grid keeps its shape rather than reflowing ragged);
- each surviving swatch's `title` gains the usage roster — every player who used the colour and
  how many times, ordered by usage (`#00BF00 — Père Castor ×5, DarkDog78 ×3`);
- review colours with no cell in the fixed grid are appended as extra swatches at the end.

Attribution is deliberately inclusive: **every** colour span counts toward its post's author,
quoted or not, and the tooltip lists everyone ordered by usage. Usage-ordering surfaces a
colour's primary owner without pretending to distinguish authored from quoted.

Insertion is split by origin, mirroring [[0017-keyboard-shortcuts-delegate-to-toolbar]]: the
**native swatches keep phpBB's own click handler** (we never touch how they insert), while the
**appended swatches carry our own handler** through the undo-safe `insertAtRange`
([[0013-undo-safe-text-insertion]]) — phpBB never bound them, since it regenerates the grid
before we inject.

Because `registerPalette` may replace the grid after we install, the filter is **installed
idempotently and re-installed by a `MutationObserver`** on `#color_palette_placeholder`; our own
writes happen while the observer is disconnected, so it only fires for phpBB's rebuild. All
palette DOM knowledge (the placeholder, the swatches, the tbody) lives in `@/lib/phpbb`
([[0005-centralize-phpbb-dom]]); the colour maths (canonicalise, aggregate, partition) is pure
and unit-tested in `palette-filter.ts`. The feature owns no persisted data — only its on/off flag
— so there is no options-page surface and no settings panel.

## Consequences

- No new toolbar button, and the native swatches behave exactly as they always did — including
  for any colour BBCode an admin redefines. We inherit phpBB's insertion for them wholesale,
  the same trade accepted in [[0017-keyboard-shortcuts-delegate-to-toolbar]].
- The rich "who used it, ordered by usage" information the first design planned to show as a list
  survives only as a **hover tooltip** — the only thing that fits a 15×12px swatch. Someone who
  wants it always-visible would need a different surface.
- A review colour outside the fixed grid is reachable only via an **appended** swatch; that path
  is ours, so it is the one place the feature writes to the textarea and the one place undo-safety
  is our responsibility rather than phpBB's.
- We are now coupled to phpBB's palette internals: the ids `#colour_palette` /
  `#color_palette_placeholder`, the `data-color` attribute, and the fact that the grid is
  regenerated once. A skin that renames those, or regenerates repeatedly, degrades the feature to
  a no-op (checkbox present, nothing filtered) rather than misfiring — and [[0005-centralize-phpbb-dom]]
  means one file to fix.
- The checkbox state is ephemeral (unticked on load). Persisting it would mean a feature-owned
  store ([[0012-feature-owned-data-stores]]); deferred until it's asked for.
