# NNNN. Short title of the decision

Status: Proposed | Accepted | Superseded by [NNNN](NNNN-....md)

Date: YYYY-MM-DD

## Context

What is the situation, force, or problem that makes a decision necessary? State the
constraints and the options that were on the table — enough that a future reader
understands *why* a choice had to be made, without needing outside knowledge.

## Decision

The decision, in active voice: "We will …". Be specific about what was chosen.

## Consequences

What becomes easier or harder as a result. Include the trade-offs accepted, not just
the upsides — the follow-on obligations, the things this now rules out, and anything a
future change would have to reckon with.
